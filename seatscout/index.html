        How it works

        1. 01 VideoA café's CCTV. Here: a sample clip, your webcam, or a file.
        2. 02 DetectYOLO11n finds people, and things like bags, laptops, cups and phones.
        3. 03 AssignEach seat is a zone on the image. A person counts if they cover a quarter of it.
        4. 04 DecideFree, Taken, Away or Check, with a clock per seat and a grace period.
        5. 05 ShowThe layout updates live. Staff get a nudge when a seat needs a look.

        Seat rules: seats.js. Detector: detector.js, YOLO11n by Ultralytics (AGPL-3.0), exported to ONNX and run with ONNX Runtime Web. Vision pipeline and seat rules designed by Dong Anh Nguyen; the 2024 detector was built by Srihari Murali. This page re-implements it for the browser.
